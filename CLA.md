## How to sign

Comment this on your pull request (or add it to the description):

> I have read the Shuttle CLA (version 1.0) and agree to its terms.

The maintainers record each signature (GitHub username, name, date, CLA version) before merging. Contributing on behalf of a company needs a separate company agreement; open an issue first.

Separately, sign off each commit (`git commit -s`) with your real name and email. The `Signed-off-by:` line certifies the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/) for that commit.
