# Shuttle commercial license

Shuttle is free under the [GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). If you cannot meet the AGPL, you can buy a commercial license instead.

## When you need one

You need a commercial license if you want to do any of these without publishing your complete source under the AGPL:

- ship a modified Shuttle, or a product that includes Shuttle code, to customers;
- run a modified Shuttle as a hosted or paid service;
- white-label, rebrand or embed Shuttle in a closed product.

## When you do not

You do **not** need one to use Shuttle personally or inside your company without distributing it, to contribute, to fork it, or to sell or host it while publishing your source under the AGPL. The AGPL text decides the exact conditions; this page only summarizes them.

## Terms

Terms are agreed per customer in a signed written agreement. Nothing on this page is an offer, a price or a grant of rights. The usual structure is:

- a flat annual fee, or a revenue share on income earned from the product built on Shuttle;
- a grant for the Shuttle code the project owner holds or may relicense (see the [CLA](CLA.md)), without the AGPL source-sharing duty;
- no relicensing of third-party components. Their own licenses still apply (see [docs/licensing.md](docs/licensing.md));
- no right to the Shuttle name or logo unless the agreement says so.
