Shuttle brings WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Messenger, Instagram, Matrix and email into a single fast inbox. It runs on your computer, keeps your data there, and doesn't need a Shuttle account.

Built for people who are tired of tab overload

Most multi-messenger apps run a full browser for every service. Shuttle doesn't, so your laptop stays quiet and your battery lasts.

Light

Shuttle uses your system's own web view instead of bundling a browser for each service. Idle networks go to sleep after a few minutes and wake when you open them.

Private

Messages and sessions are stored on your machine. Passwords go in your operating system's keyring. Usage statistics are off unless you turn them on.

Calm

One place to check instead of seven. Quiet hours, mute, archive and priority groups let you decide what deserves your attention and when.

What you can do with it

Everything is designed to cut the time you spend switching apps.

One unified inbox

Every chat from every network and account in one list, with search, unread badges, and pin, mute and archive.

Several accounts, side by side

Run two WhatsApps, a work Telegram and a personal one together. Sign in the way each network expects: QR code, phone and code, or password.

Your data stays yours

No Shuttle server sits between you and your messages. Back up and restore with a password-protected export.

Notifications you control

Native desktop alerts with quiet hours and per-account or per-chat mute. Read receipts stay off until you switch them on.

Stay organised

Workspaces, priority groups, notes on any chat (with a timer or alarm if you want one), and to-dos that quote the message they came from.

Send smarter

Forward between chats or accounts, schedule a message for later, or delay send so you have a moment to change your mind.

Media done properly

Play video and audio in the thread, open photos in a lightbox, and edit an image before you send it. Saved files never overwrite older ones.

Looks the way you like

Follows your system's light or dark mode, ships with theme presets, and accepts your own CSS if you want to go further.

Made for daily use

Lives in the system tray, jumps between chats with Ctrl/Cmd+K, and has keyboard shortcuts for the things you do all day.

Make it yours with tweakcn themes

Shuttle follows your system's light or dark mode out of the box. If you want a different look, paste a theme from tweakcn and the whole app changes.

Shuttle Zinc Ocean Twilight Any tweakcn theme
  1. Pick a theme on tweakcn.com, or design your own with its visual editor.
  2. Paste its share link or theme ID into Shuttle's appearance settings.
  3. Done. Colours, chat bubbles, accents and Google Fonts follow the theme, in light and dark.

Every network you use

Each network runs in its own separate process, so one misbehaving service can't take down the rest. Components download the first time you add an account for that network.
